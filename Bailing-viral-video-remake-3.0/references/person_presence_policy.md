# Person Presence Policy

Use this policy whenever a storyboard or final video prompt includes people.

## Core Rule

Do not use sketch, oil-painting, blur, crop, partial-face wording, or any other style conversion as a way to bypass face-review or face-reference restrictions. The final video prompt must describe the intended scene truthfully.

Storyboard panels may use sketch, line-art, or painterly planning language only as internal director visualization. Do not transfer that wording into the final Seedance prompt as an evasion method.

## Seedance Prompt Rule

If a person is necessary for product context, write direct compliant language such as:

- `one anonymous adult lifestyle model`
- `realistic commercial look`
- `product remains the main subject`
- `no celebrity likeness`
- `no identity continuity required across shots`
- `hands, torso, shoulder, back view, or partial lifestyle presence only when appropriate`

Do not upload or rely on a face reference image for Seedance. If a provided reference image contains a face, do not use it as an identity reference. Crop, omit, or replace it with product-only references when needed.

## Product-First Guidance

- Beauty and skincare: hands, neck/shoulder, jawline edge, or one anonymous adult lifestyle model if needed.
- Apparel and accessories: body fit, hands, torso, walking or wearing context; face should not be the continuity anchor.
- Home, kitchen, food, electronics: hands and lifestyle interaction are usually enough.
- Intimate apparel: product-only, mannequin, flat lay, hanger, fabric handling, or anonymous non-sexual product demonstration. Avoid exposed body, sexualized framing, or full-face model focus.

## Stability Guidance

- Keep people secondary to the product.
- Use at most one person-heavy shot unless the product truly requires a model.
- Do not require the same person's face across shots.
- Do not ask for facial expression acting unless it is essential and allowed by the model and product category.
- Prefer product, hand, material, fit, and usage details over face-led storytelling.
