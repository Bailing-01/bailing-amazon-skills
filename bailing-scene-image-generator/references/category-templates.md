# Category Templates

Use this file after `scene-matching.md` when the product category is recognizable. These templates define the default eight-image sales tasks by category. Adapt them to the user's product and scene request; do not force a template when it would conflict with visible product truth.

## How To Use

1. Pick the closest category template.
2. Preserve the user's explicit scene, audience, and platform choices.
3. Replace any role that does not fit the exact product with a role that proves use, fit, scale, material, storage, installation, comfort, taste, occasion, or brand mood.
4. Keep one unified campaign visual system across all eight images.

## Apparel

Use for dresses, tops, pants, coats, activewear, underwear, and wearable fashion.

- Hero wearing scene: full garment visible, strongest style/occasion match.
- Fit proof: front or three-quarter view showing neckline, shoulder/sleeve/strap, waist, hem, and silhouette.
- Detail construction: fabric texture, seam, pleat, pocket, closure, trim, knit, or drape.
- Movement or natural posture: walking, turning, sitting, stretching, or daily movement that proves comfort and fabric behavior.
- Back or side view: structure, length, volume, and coverage.
- Occasion scene: event, commute, vacation, home, workout, or weather context matched to the garment.
- Social lifestyle: target buyer's natural shareable moment while product remains dominant.
- Brand mood cover: strongest campaign image with clear product silhouette.

Avoid changing garment length, sleeve/strap design, fit type, fabric category, or color. If no measurements are provided, use standard adult body proportion and category-normal fit.

## Footwear And Sportswear

Use for shoes, sandals, boots, socks, sports apparel, and training accessories.

- Hero worn or placed on product-matched surface.
- Side profile and silhouette proof.
- On-foot scale and fit.
- Movement use: running, walking, training, warm-up, commute, trail, or sport-specific action.
- Material/detail close-up: upper, sole, laces, tread, cushioning, closure, textile.
- Functional scene: grip, flexibility, comfort, breathability, or support only when visually inferable without claims.
- Social lifestyle: clean athletic or street scene matched to style.
- Brand mood cover: high-impact but product-readable sport or lifestyle campaign image.

Avoid unsupported performance claims, impossible foot bend, wrong shoe pair orientation, unrelated luxury interiors, or dirty scenes that make the product look old unless requested.

## Bags And Accessories

Use for handbags, backpacks, wallets, belts, hats, scarves, jewelry, watches, eyewear, and similar accessories.

- Hero carry or styled placement.
- Body-scale proof: hand, shoulder, waist, head, wrist, or face scale depending on product.
- Outfit pairing scene matched to audience and occasion.
- Detail close-up: hardware, texture, stitching, clasp, strap, zipper, frame, lens, stone, dial.
- Use interaction: opening, holding, wearing, adjusting, packing, or gifting.
- Spatial display: vanity, entryway, boutique, desk, travel, car, or closet context.
- Social lifestyle: natural wearing/carrying moment.
- Brand mood cover: strong silhouette and product-tier expression.

Avoid implying storage capacity, included items, precious-metal grade, waterproofing, or certification unless provided.

## Beauty And Personal Care

Use for skincare, makeup, fragrance, haircare, tools, wellness-adjacent personal goods, and packaging-led beauty products.

- Hero packaging scene with clean product readability.
- Handheld scale proof.
- Vanity/bathroom routine scene.
- Texture or application detail when product type supports it.
- Ingredient/sensory atmosphere only when ingredients are visible or provided.
- Travel pouch, shelf, mirror, or gifting context.
- Social lifestyle: clean routine moment with human warmth.
- Brand mood cover: premium beauty still life or model-led beauty scene.

Avoid medical settings, treatment claims, before/after claims, fake lab tests, fake dermatologist approval, invented ingredients, or over-saturated messy bathrooms.

## Home Goods And Soft Furnishing

Use for curtains, bedding, rugs, organizers, furniture, lamps, decor, cleaning, storage, and household products.

- Hero room placement with believable scale.
- Functional use scene: sitting, opening, storing, cleaning, lighting, covering, organizing, or touching.
- Detail texture/material close-up.
- Installation, hanging, placement, or access detail when relevant.
- Scale proof against furniture, wall, door, window, bed, table, or human body.
- Room mood scene matched to product style.
- Social lifestyle/home moment where product remains visible.
- Brand mood cover with strong interior styling.

Avoid room dimensions, installation claims, material claims, or before/after transformation that the user did not provide.

## Kitchen, Food, Drink, And Tableware

Use for cookware, utensils, cups, dishes, food packaging, snacks, drinks, and dining products.

- Hero table or counter scene.
- Hand/use scale proof.
- Preparation or serving moment.
- Texture/detail close-up.
- Storage or packaging scene.
- Occasion scene: breakfast, lunch, dinner, picnic, party, cafe, office snack, or family table as appropriate.
- Social lifestyle dining or sharing moment.
- Brand mood cover with appetizing but truthful styling.

Avoid invented ingredients, health claims, fake nutrition claims, fake awards, or serving situations that contradict the product.

## Electronics, Tools, And Functional Goods

Use for small electronics, desk products, tools, equipment, devices, cases, mounts, and functional accessories.

- Hero product in clean use environment.
- Hand scale or operation scene.
- Interface/port/control detail only if visible or provided.
- Workflow scene: desk, workshop, travel, car, kitchen, bathroom, garden, or sport use as appropriate.
- Storage/charging/setup scene when supported.
- Function proof without unsupported claims.
- Social lifestyle or productivity scene.
- Brand mood cover with technical clarity.

Avoid screens with invented UI, fake specs, fake app features, unsafe handling, unsupported waterproofing, or unprovided compatibility claims.

## Ambiguous Or Mixed Category

When the category is unclear:

- prefer close product-led scenes, hand/body/table scale, material detail, and neutral use contexts;
- avoid wide scenes that require exact size, installation, function, or target audience assumptions;
- state conservative category-normal relative scale in the prompt;
- do not invent claims or accessories to make a scene work.
